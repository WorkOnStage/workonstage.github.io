01 / WHY WE EXIST

More than a title.
More than a CV.

Behind every paper, product, experiment, and breakthrough is a person with a story worth hearing.

WorkOnStage gives STEM professionals a space to explain what they do in their own words, share what excites them, and connect with people who might see the world a little differently.

A spotlight on the work. A bridge between the people. ↗
02 / THE FORMAT

Simple format.
Big conversations.

Less jargon. More curiosity. Just enough structure to let the interesting stuff happen.

ACT 01
10min

The spotlight

A high-level introduction to the guest, the questions driving their work, and why it matters. No specialist background required.

↗
ACT 02
30min

The conversation

An open Q&A about ideas, challenges, careers, collaborations, and the unexpected paths that brought them here.
